## Key Interactions to Verify
- Mom sends a Hinglish voice note and gets back a readback voice note plus a text listing every item and the total (WhatsApp).
- "haan beta bhej do" counts as yes; "haan aur chai patti bhi" goes back to the draft (WhatsApp).
- The child taps Approve COD, and Mom hears "Rahul ne bhej diya ... ₹{total} dena hai" (Telegram → WhatsApp).
- NEEDS_REVIEW: the "Order aaya" and "Nahi hua" buttons resolve it, and the automatic re-check at +5 minutes resolves a matching order (Telegram).
- First contact: Mom gets the one-time recording notice; "band karo" wipes her data (WhatsApp).
- A message from a stranger's number is ignored, and the child gets one notice per day (WhatsApp → Telegram).

## Edge Cases
- Meta delivers the same voice note twice: only one draft is created.
- Two quick notes ("doodh", then "aur bread bhi"): both items end up in the cart and the readback matches the cart.
- Mom stays silent: one reminder at 30 minutes, then cancel at 2 hours.
- The laptop sleeps past the 2 hours: only one cancel message is sent, never reminder-then-cancel.
- Mom's late "haan" arrives after her draft was cancelled while offline: she's told it was cancelled and asked again.
- A note arrives more than 30 minutes late: Mom is asked "ab bhi chahiye?", and several late notes are merged into one question.
- The cart is under ₹99 and Mom declines an add-on: the order is cancelled and Mom is told.
- Checkout times out after committing: goes to NEEDS_REVIEW, never two orders. A connection refused before sending is retried once.
- Checkout comes back out of stock or unserviceable: goes to DRAFT or FAILED, and Mom gets an explanation.
- A 401 mid-flow: the draft pauses, the child re-authenticates, and the draft resumes.
- The server restarts during CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING or PLACING(COD): the lease is reclaimed and exactly one order is placed.
- Sarvam or Claude is down, Claude refuses, the media fetch fails, or TTS fails: Mom gets a visible message (or a text-only reply), and the child gets the error class and session id.
- The readback takes more than 8 seconds: one interim "sun liya, ek minute" is sent.

## Critical Paths
- COD journey: voice note, then readback, then "haan", then the child approves COD, then checkout, then the PLACED note with the amount.
- Restart journey: kill the process mid-approval or mid-placing, restart, and exactly one order is placed.
- `scripts/preflight.py` shows every check as PASS before filming.
- Agent eval (`evals/cart_eval.py`): at least 22 of 25 correct before any prompt change and before filming.
