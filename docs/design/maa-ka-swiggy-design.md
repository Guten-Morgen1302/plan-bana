## Problem Statement

Indian parents who don't use shopping apps get groceries by calling their kids, who then order on Swiggy from another city. The parent can't self-serve, the child becomes a human order-relay, and every "doodh khatam ho gaya" costs a phone call and a context switch.

Goal of the project (the real one): a demo so good that Swiggy's Builders Club team features it and their recruiting team reaches out for an internship. Swiggy's own words on what they hire for: "strong engineering thinking, product sense, and creativity"; a good demo shows "the full loop: a real use case, the agent workflow, and a working demo."

## What Makes This Cool

- **Zero new behaviour for the parent.** Mom sends a WhatsApp voice note in Hinglish, exactly like she already does to family: "doodh, bread, ande bhej do." She gets a voice note back. No app, no links, no IDs, no typing.
- **The user is the family pair, not the elder alone.** Parent speaks; the adult child (remote, other city) is the payer and approver. The order runs on the child's Swiggy account and delivers to Mom's address (`create_address` supports `receiverName`/`receiverPhone`). On the UPI path the payment link goes to the child, not Mom.
- **Trust by design.** Voice-clone scams on seniors are rising. Mom never makes a digital payment and never approves spend; the only money she can touch is cash on a child-approved COD order at her own door. The LLM can never spend money at all: order placement is code-only, gated on two human approvals bound to an exact cart.
- **Real-world close.** Real parent, real order, real doorbell, on camera.
- **Likely open niche (from a brief search, not exhaustive).** Found: Flipkart + Sarvam voice WhatsApp ordering for kirana retailers (B2B) [mitsloanme.com/article/flipkart-brings-voice-led-wholesale-ordering-to-whatsapp-with-sarvam-ai], generic WhatsApp commerce bots [egrow.com 2026 playbook], senior-focused app lists recommending regular grocery apps [sparkcanvas.in]. None found targeting elders with remote-child payment.

## Constraints

- **Timebox:** 1-2 weeks, solo, then email demo to builders@swiggy.in.
- **No sandbox.** Swiggy MCP "localhost" dev hits PRODUCTION with the builder's real Swiggy account; `checkout` places real orders. `DRY_RUN` + a fake MCP server (below) from day 2.
- **Auth:** OAuth 2.1 + PKCE (phone + OTP in a browser), Dynamic Client Registration at `POST /auth/register`, 5-day access token, **no refresh tokens in v1**, 30-day sliding session. Re-auth needs a browser, so the child must do it.
- **Rate limits:** 70 req/min/user/server, 30/min for write tools, 2x burst over 10s. One persistent MCP session; never reconnect per call; init servers sequentially.
- **Instamart:** ₹99 minimum; one account-level cart bound to an address (`clear_cart` before switching); `update_cart` REPLACES the whole cart; `checkout` is **not idempotent**.
- **Payments (headless UPI):** `checkout(paymentMethod:"UPI", intentApp|generateUPIQR)` creates the order in `PENDING_PAYMENT` and returns `data.bridgeUrl` + `paasId` + `orderId` + `pollingIntervalInMs` + `maxTimeToPollForInMs`. So payment always happens AFTER checkout. `check_payment_status` is a ~19s long-poll; cap the loop; at cap-while-pending call `confirm_order` once (Instamart: `orderId` + `paasId`); `confirm_order` is idempotent. Never ask for a UPI ID (NPCI). COD (`paymentMethod:"Cash"`) has no payment leg.
- **Voice guardrails (Swiggy docs):** ≤3 items read aloud, prices spoken in rupees, never read IDs, always confirm before placing.
- **WhatsApp Cloud API test number:** 5 verified recipients; 24h customer-service window. Mom always initiates (voice note), so replies to her are inside the window. Messages to the child are usually business-initiated and need approved templates; test-number template support is unverified (see Open Question 5 and the child-channel fallback).
- **Data (DPDP 2023):** Swiggy is the Data Fiduciary; this app is a processor. See "Data handling" below.
- **Prod-access application** (Google Form) requires a production HTTPS redirect URI, tech-stack overview, a demo video link "viewable without requesting access" (unlisted YouTube satisfies this), expected volume, and ToS acknowledgement.

## Premises

1. The user is the FAMILY PAIR: parent speaks on WhatsApp; the child is payer + approver. Runs on the child's Swiggy account, delivering to the parent's address.
2. Parent's side has zero new behaviour: voice note in, voice note + short text out; never a link, ID, or number to deal with.
3. Instamart "the usual" (`your_go_to_items`) is the v1 hero flow, backed by a local "Maa's list" and 2-3 seed orders before filming (revised after second-opinion challenge). Food is v2.
4. Safety over speed: no order without (a) Mom's unambiguous spoken yes on a specific cart snapshot AND (b) the child's approval of that same snapshot (Approve COD, or Pay UPI which then triggers checkout + payment link). Check-then-retry idempotency on `checkout` is non-negotiable; ambiguous outcomes fail closed.
5. Demo = real parent, real order, real doorbell, on camera, plus a clean GitHub repo with setup instructions.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Scripted pipeline (LLM only parses)
Voice → STT → one LLM call to a structured item list → fixed-order MCP calls → TTS reply. Most reliable and fastest (S), but weak "AI-native" story and brittle off-script. Not chosen; its fixed path is reused as the days 1-2 skeleton.

### Approach B: Guarded agent + code-only commit (CHOSEN)
Claude agent with a small allowlist owns the Hinglish conversation; a deterministic commit layer owns address, cart binding, approvals and money. Details below.

### Approach C: "Maa is okay" rhythm agent
Proactive cadence-learning care agent. Not chosen for v1: can't honestly show a learned rhythm in 2 weeks, and proactive messages need templates. Kept as the pitch's "what's next" slide.

## Recommended Approach

**Approach B: Guarded agent + code-only commit.** Chosen because it maps to what Swiggy says it hires for (engineering thinking + product sense), fits 1-2 weeks, and ends ready to apply for production access.

### Stack (default; switchable to TypeScript)
Python 3.12, FastAPI (webhook), pywa (WhatsApp Cloud API), official `mcp` SDK (streamable HTTP client to `https://mcp.swiggy.com/im`), Claude API for the agent, Sarvam Saaras v3 (STT) + Bulbul v3 (TTS), ffmpeg (→ OGG/Opus ≤512 KB), SQLite, cloudflared tunnel for dev, one small VPS with HTTPS (Caddy) for the demo + prod redirect URI.

### Architecture

```
Mom's WhatsApp ──voice note──► Webhook (FastAPI + pywa)
                                   │ download media → Sarvam STT (Hinglish)
                                   │ family lock check (one active order per family)
                                   ▼
                        Conversation agent (Claude)
                        allowlisted tools ONLY:
                          your_go_to_items, search_products (read)
                          propose_cart(items)  ← local tool; code calls update_cart
                          maa_list (local read)
                        addressId is injected by code, never chosen by the LLM
                                   │ proposed cart
                                   ▼
                  Commit layer (plain code, SQLite ledger + state machine)
```

### Address and cart binding (code-owned)
- **Mom's `addressId` is pinned in config** (created once via `create_address` in the day-1 script, with `receiverName`/`receiverPhone` = Mom). `get_addresses`, `create_address`, `delete_address` are not in the LLM allowlist.
- **At the start of every draft, code runs `clear_cart` then binds the cart to Mom's address.** All `update_cart` calls go through the local `propose_cart` tool, which injects Mom's `addressId`. The pre-commit `get_cart` must show Mom's address or the commit is rejected.
- **Shared-account contention:** the child's own Instamart use would overwrite Mom's cart (one cart per account). Mitigation for v1: the child doesn't use Instamart on this account while a Mom draft is active (bot warns the child), and the demo uses the child's account only for Mom. v2: a dedicated family account.
- `list_coupons` is dropped from v1 (no coupon step in the commit layer).

### Commit protocol (state machine)

Ledger key: `family_id` + local `draft_id`. Each approval stores a `cart_hash` = sha256(sorted spinId:qty pairs + bill total). A unique DB constraint allows at most one row per family in {`CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING`, `PLACING`, `PAYMENT_PENDING`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`}.

```
DRAFT ──(agent proposes cart; code reads back)──► AWAITING_PARENT
AWAITING_PARENT ──(unambiguous yes on snapshot H1)──► CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING
AWAITING_PARENT ──(modifying/mixed reply)──► DRAFT
CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING ──[Approve COD, hash==H1]──► PLACING(COD) ──► PLACED
CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING ──[Pay UPI, hash==H1]──► PLACING(UPI) ──► PAYMENT_PENDING
PAYMENT_PENDING ──(success/paid)──► PLACED
PAYMENT_PENDING ──(failed)──► CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING (child offered: retry UPI / switch to COD / cancel)
PAYMENT_PENDING ──(cancelled | refund-initiated)──► CANCELLED
PAYMENT_PENDING ──(cart_changed)──► DRAFT (approvals invalidated; Mom re-confirms)
PAYMENT_PENDING ──(cap hit → confirm_order still pending)──► NEEDS_REVIEW
CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING ──[Cancel] or 2h expiry──► CANCELLED
PLACING ──(ambiguous outcome after reconciliation)──► NEEDS_REVIEW
```

- **Parent confirmation:** the readback voice note says up to 3 items aloud ("aur 2 cheezein") plus the total; the accompanying short text lists ALL items and the total, so the confirmation covers the full cart. The STT transcript is classified by code, not the LLM: a small fixed yes-lexicon ("haan", "haa", "ha ji", "theek hai", "bhej do", "yes", "ok") with no other content words → yes; a fixed no-lexicon → cancel; anything else (e.g. "haan, aur chai patti bhi") → back to DRAFT with the new request passed to the agent. Code records `H1` at that moment.
- **Child approval:** approval messages carry `draft_id` + `H1` in the button payload. The handler rejects if the sender isn't the registered child number, the `draft_id` isn't the active draft, or `H1` mismatches.
- **Pre-commit re-check:** right before `checkout`, code calls `get_cart`, recomputes the hash. If it differs from `H1`: item/quantity change → back to DRAFT (Mom re-confirms); price-only change within ₹20 → proceed; above ₹20 → child re-approves.
- **Family lock:** while a draft is in `CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING` / `PLACING` / `PAYMENT_PENDING`, the agent cannot write the cart. A new voice note from Mom is queued and she hears "pichla order abhi confirm ho raha hai, uske baad yeh bhi mangwa dete hain." After the active order resolves, the queued note starts a new draft.
- **Approval expiry:** child gets a reminder at 30 min; at 2h the draft is CANCELLED, code runs `clear_cart`, Mom hears "Rahul abhi busy hain, thodi der mein phir se bolna."
- **Outcome messages:** PLACED → Mom: "Rahul ne bhej diya, {deliveryTimeSpoken} mein aayega"; child: order summary. `failed` → child choice, Mom hears nothing new until resolved. CANCELLED → both told in one line. NEEDS_REVIEW → child gets the order id + "check the Swiggy app"; Mom hears "thoda ruko."

### Idempotency and retries
- **Generic MCP wrapper** (backoff 500ms → 8s with jitter, max 5, honour `Retry-After`) applies to reads and `update_cart`/`clear_cart` only. **`checkout` and `confirm_order` are never retried by the wrapper.**
- **Check-then-retry for `checkout`:** on 5xx/network error, wait 5s, then `get_orders` up to 3 times over ~30s. Match rule: returned `orderId` if the failed call yielded one; otherwise an order to Mom's address created after the attempt's start time whose item set + total equal `H1`'s. Exactly one match → treat as success. No match after 30s → retry once. Anything ambiguous (multiple candidates, partial match) → NEEDS_REVIEW, no auto-retry.
- **UPI path:** the same reconciliation applies to `checkout(UPI)`. `confirm_order` is idempotent, so it may be retried via the wrapper exception list only after the payment worker decides to finalize.

### Maa's list (fallback "usual")
- Table: `phrase` (normalized transcript token, e.g. "doodh") → `spinId`, `skuId`, `last_ordered_at`, `count`. Written only when a draft reaches PLACED, one row per item using the phrase the agent matched.
- Lookup order per requested phrase: (1) Maa's list hit that is in stock per `search_products`; (2) `your_go_to_items` entry whose name matches the phrase; (3) `search_products` top result → agent asks Mom to pick variant (max 2 choices spoken).
- "Relevant" = matches a requested phrase and is in stock at Mom's address.

### Voice handling
- Readback shaping: `shortDescription` / spoken-friendly fields, max 3 items aloud, rupees spoken naturally, never IDs.
- **Low-confidence STT:** if Saaras confidence is below a threshold (tuned on the benchmark set) or no item phrase is recognised, Mom hears "samajh nahi aaya, ek baar phir bolna"; after 2 failures, the transcript is forwarded to the child as text for manual help.
- **Non-voice input:** text messages from Mom go through the same pipeline minus STT; images/stickers get "bolke bata do na" (voice prompt). Voice notes over 60s are truncated to the first 60s for STT, with a warning to the child.

### Domain edge cases
- **Out of stock:** agent offers up to 2 alternatives from `search_products` for that item ("Amul nahi hai, Mother Dairy chalega?"); if none or Mom says no, the item is dropped and the readback says so.
- **Unserviceable address:** Mom hears "abhi aapke ghar Instamart nahi aa raha"; the child gets the raw reason. No order.
- **Under ₹99:** the agent asks Mom once "kuch aur chahiye?" and suggests one Maa's-list item; if she says no, the child is told the order can't be placed below ₹99.

### Token lifecycle
- Access token + `expires_at` stored encrypted at rest (key from env). At T-24h the child gets a re-auth link to `/auth/start` (PKCE, DCR client).
- On 401 / JSON-RPC -32001 mid-flow: the draft is persisted with its state; Mom hears "thoda ruko, Rahul se confirm kar rahe hain"; the child gets the re-auth link. When `/auth/start` completes, the paused draft resumes from its persisted state (approvals re-validated against a fresh `get_cart` hash). Drafts paused more than 2h are cancelled with a note to Mom.

### Child channel
- Preferred: WhatsApp (same bot) using approved templates for: approval with buttons, UPI payment link, re-auth nudge, failure/NEEDS_REVIEW notice.
- **Fallback if the test number can't send these templates:** the child side runs on a Telegram bot (bots can message a user any time after `/start`, inline buttons included). Mom's side stays on WhatsApp. This is decided on day 3 (Open Question 5).

### Observability and support
- JSON log per tool call: `session_id`, tool, duration, status, hashed family id. No request/response bodies.
- `report_error` is Swiggy's MCP tool (returns a pre-filled mailto link). Code calls it on any NEEDS_REVIEW or after 3 consecutive failed tool calls in one draft, and sends the link to the child.

### Data handling (DPDP)
- Sub-processors: Sarvam (Mom's voice audio for STT, reply text for TTS), Anthropic (transcripts, item names, cart summary; no phone numbers or addresses), Meta WhatsApp (messages).
- Retention: inbound audio and generated TTS audio deleted after 24h; transcripts kept 7 days for debugging, then deleted; the 10-note STT benchmark set is recorded with Mom's consent, stored locally only, and excluded from the repo. Maa's list keeps only item phrases and SKU ids.
- Account deletion: a `/forget` command from the child wipes the family's rows, audio and tokens and calls `POST /auth/logout`.

### Testing without a sandbox
- **Fake MCP server** (in-repo) replays Day-1 recorded fixtures and supports fault injection: 5xx on checkout, timeout after commit, 401, out-of-stock, unserviceable, under-₹99, `cart_changed`, payment `failed`/pending-past-cap. CI runs the state-machine and idempotency tests against it.
- **Production tests are a named, budgeted set:** the day-1 probe order, 2 seed orders, 1 UPI order, and 3 filmed demo orders (≈7 orders, ≈₹1,500 total, all real groceries Mom uses). No fault injection against production. Accidental real orders: cancel in the Swiggy app immediately or keep them (they're groceries Mom uses anyway).
- `DRY_RUN=1` default outside demo runs: the commit layer logs the would-be `checkout` and stops.

## Reviewer Concerns

Disposition: CONCERNS_RECORDED

Stop: CONVERGENCE

### R2-1 — feasibility

**Problem**

> Check-then-retry still auto-retries checkout when get_orders shows no match after about 30s ('No match after 30s -> retry once'). No match is not proof that no order exists: propagation delay is unknown (Open Question 6), and checkout is not idempotent. A late-appearing order plus the retry gives two orders. This conflicts with Premise 4 ('ambiguous outcomes fail closed') and the zero-duplicate success criterion.

**Remedy**

> Treat 'no match within the window' as ambiguous. Go to NEEDS_REVIEW and ask the child, with no auto-retry, unless the failure is provably pre-commit (for example, connection refused before the request was sent). Alternatively, make the auto-retry conditional on the day-1 measured propagation bound from Open Question 6, and document that bound.

### R2-2 — consistency

**Problem**

> The reconciliation match rule requires the order's item set + total to equal H1's. But the pre-commit re-check lets checkout proceed when the price changes by up to ₹20, and a child re-approval above ₹20 produces a different total. In those cases the real order's total differs from H1, reconciliation finds no match, and the path falls through to retry, risking a duplicate order.

**Remedy**

> Match reconciliation against the hash/snapshot actually submitted to checkout (the pre-commit get_cart result stored on the attempt row), not H1. State which total (item subtotal or bill including fees) is compared against get_orders.

### R2-3 — feasibility

**Problem**

> On PAYMENT_PENDING -> failed, the child is offered 'retry UPI / switch to COD'. The first UPI checkout already created an order (orderId, PENDING_PAYMENT). The design doesn't say whether that order is cancelled, expires, or can be re-paid, or whether the cart still exists after checkout. A fresh checkout for the retry or COD switch could create a second order, or fail the pre-commit hash check because the cart was consumed.

**Remedy**

> Define the retry mechanics: whether a retry re-uses the existing orderId/paasId (new bridgeUrl) or needs a new checkout, and how the prior PENDING_PAYMENT order is confirmed dead before any new checkout (for example, via get_orders or track_order). Also define the cart state after a failed payment (rebuild from snapshot vs re-read). Add this to Open Question 4 and the fake-server fault tests.

### R2-4 — consistency

**Problem**

> confirm_order retry policy is contradictory. Constraints say to call confirm_order 'once' at cap-while-pending. Idempotency says 'checkout and confirm_order are never retried by the wrapper'. The UPI path says confirm_order 'may be retried via the wrapper exception list'.

**Remedy**

> Pick one rule (for example, confirm_order is called by the payment worker and may be retried up to N times with backoff because it is idempotent; checkout is never wrapper-retried) and state it consistently in all three places.

### R2-5 — consistency

**Problem**

> The pre-commit re-check outcomes are missing from the state diagram: back to DRAFT on item change, proceed on a price change within ₹20, child re-approval above ₹20. These transitions happen during or after the child's approval (CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING/PLACING). cart_hash is a single sha256 over spinId:qty + total, so code can't tell an item change from a price-only change from the hash alone. The re-approval path doesn't say which new hash the child's button is bound to, even though the approval handler rejects anything that doesn't match H1.

**Remedy**

> Store the full snapshot (items, qty, prices, total) with each approval, not only the hash. Add the re-check transitions to the diagram, and define the re-approval as a new hash H2 carried in a new button payload that supersedes H1.

### R2-6 — completeness

**Problem**

> PLACING has no transition for definitive (non-ambiguous) checkout errors: 4xx validation, an item going out of stock at checkout, unserviceable at checkout, a below-minimum total. Only success and 'ambiguous outcome after reconciliation' are defined, so a clean business error would either be treated as ambiguous (NEEDS_REVIEW) or leave the draft stuck in PLACING.

**Remedy**

> Add PLACING -> FAILED (or back to DRAFT/CHILD_APPROVAL_PENDING) for definitive errors, with the message to the child and Mom, and say that these errors skip reconciliation.

### R2-7 — completeness

**Problem**

> NEEDS_REVIEW has no exit. It sits in the per-family unique constraint, and queued voice notes wait until the active order 'resolves', so one NEEDS_REVIEW blocks all future orders for the family indefinitely. Nothing says how the child marks it resolved (placed/not placed), what Mom hears after 'thoda ruko', or whether the family lock covers NEEDS_REVIEW (the lock text lists only three states).

**Remedy**

> Define a child action (for example, buttons 'order aaya / order nahi aaya' or a command) that moves NEEDS_REVIEW to PLACED or CANCELLED, with a follow-up message to Mom. Include NEEDS_REVIEW in the family-lock wording, and set a timeout or escalation.

### R2-8 — completeness

**Problem**

> AWAITING_PARENT is incomplete. The no-lexicon reply ('fixed no-lexicon -> cancel') has no transition in the diagram, and nothing covers Mom never replying: no expiry, no reminder, and the bound cart stays on the child's account indefinitely.

**Remedy**

> Add AWAITING_PARENT -> CANCELLED for a 'no' and for a stated timeout, with clear_cart and a short voice acknowledgement to Mom.

### R2-9 — clarity

**Problem**
